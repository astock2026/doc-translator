Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

When you use DocTranslator, we temporarily receive the .docx file you upload for translation processing only. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

How We Use Your Data

Your uploaded document is used solely to perform the Chinese-to-English translation. The file is processed on our servers and is automatically deleted after the translation is complete and downloaded.

Data Retention

Uploaded files and translated outputs are automatically purged from our servers within 24 hours. No copies are retained.

Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer your documents to any third party.

Cookies

DocTranslator does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
